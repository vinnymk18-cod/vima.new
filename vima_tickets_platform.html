Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay with M-PESA?

Send the exact ticket amount to official number 0740820891 via M-PESA Send Money or Paybill, then submit your 10-digit transaction code during checkout.

How does secondary resale verification work?

When a ticket is resold, our system cross-checks the original ticket ID. Upon confirmation, the original barcode is invalidated and a fresh QR code is issued to the new buyer.

Are ticket prices capped on resale?

Yes. VIMA TICKETS enforces a strict 120% face value price cap on all secondary resale listings to protect fans from aggressive scalping.
